**Cong Huang** is a domain expert with nearly ten years of experience in launch vehicle attitude control.

- **Professional Experience**: Since 2017, he has worked at the Beijing Institute of Aerospace Automatic Control, affiliated with the China Academy of Launch Vehicle Technology. He currently serves as a **senior engineer** and **program chief designer**, with extensive experience in the design of attitude control systems for launch vehicles and missile systems. He has been responsible for developing attitude control systems for six vehicle programs, including the *Long March 5* and *Long March 10* launch vehicles. His work has contributed to fault-tolerant control, efficient load alleviation, and adaptive control for launch vehicles, supporting major national aerospace missions such as *Chang'e 5*, *Chang'e 6*, *Tianwen 1*, and the *China Space Station*. He has also led the attitude control system studies for advanced research programs, including intelligent launch vehicles. Over the past five years, he has published 10 research papers and filed or obtained 15 patents. His honors include the First Prize of the National Defense Science and Technology Progress Award and the First Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award of his affiliated aerospace corporation.

- **Educational Background**: In 2025, he concurrently enrolled as a doctoral candidate in engineering at the College of Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. He received his master's degree in Control Engineering from the School of Automation Science and Electrical Engineering, Beihang University, in 2017, and his bachelor's degree in Automation from the same school in 2014.

- **Research Interests**: His research interests include launch vehicle attitude control, fault-tolerant control for launch vehicles, efficient load alleviation, adaptive control, and intelligent control of aerospace vehicles.
